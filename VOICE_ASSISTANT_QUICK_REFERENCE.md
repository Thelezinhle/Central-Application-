## ⚙️ Settings

**Enable/Disable Voice:**
- Click "Listen" button in widget to start
- Click "Off" button to disable voice mode

**Troubleshooting:**
- Press F12 to check browser console
- Make sure microphone is allowed
- Speak clearly and pause between commands
