# 📖 How to Use This App - Super Simple Guide

## For Students Who've Never Used An App Before

---

## 🎯 What Can I Do With This App?

This app helps you:
- ✅ Find schools and colleges
- ✅ Learn about what they offer
- ✅ Apply to schools online

**That's it! Three things.**

---

## 🏠 When You First Open The App

You'll see a **green header** with a logo.

Below that, there are buttons that say:
- "🎓 Find Your School" ← Click this one first!
- "Browse Courses" 
- "Get Recommendations"

**Just click "Find Your School" to start.**

---

## 🔍 How to Find A School (3 Easy Steps)

### STEP 1️⃣ Type The School Name

You'll see a **white box** that says "What's the name of the school?"

**Just type!** For example:
- `UNIZULU` (a university)
- `False Bay` (a training college)
- `Wits` (short for University of Witwatersrand)

The app will find it automatically. Don't worry about getting it perfect!

💡 **Tip:** If you're not sure of the name, try typing just a few letters. Like "uni" or "false".

---

### STEP 2️⃣ (Optional) Pick The School Type

Below the search box, you'll see a **dropdown** that says "School Type"

You can pick:
- 📚 "All Types" ← Easy way, just pick this!
- 🎓 University
- 🏫 College (CAO)
- 🔧 Training College
- 💼 Private College

**If you're not sure what type it is, just leave it as "All Types".**

---

### STEP 3️⃣ (Optional) Pick Your Province

Below that is another **dropdown** that says "Your Province"

Pick the province where you want to study (or leave as "All Provinces").

Provinces in South Africa:
- Western Cape
- Eastern Cape  
- Northern Cape
- South Africa
- KwaZulu-Natal
- Limpopo
- Mpumalanga
- Gauteng
- North West

## 🎬 What Do The Badges Mean?

### School Type Badges:
- 🎓 **University** = Big university with many courses
- 🏫 **College (CAO)** = Smaller college, apply through CAO
- 🔧 **Training College** = Learn practical skills (plumbing, nursing, etc)
- 💼 **Private College** = Paid college

### Government Approval Badges:
- ✓ **CHE** = Government approved (safe to apply)
- ✓ **DHET** = Government certified (skills programs)
- ✓ **SETA** = Government recognized

**Green badges = Good sign! The school is official.**

## 🔍 I Can't Find A School

**No worries!** Try this:

1. Check the **spelling** - Is it spelled correctly?
2. **Remove the province filter** - Try "All Provinces"
3. **Try the school website URL** - Google the school name
4. **Ask a parent or teacher** - They might know the exact name

You'll see a button that says **← Start Over** to try again.

---

## 💡 Tips For Success

### ✓ Search Tips:
- Type slowly and check spelling
- Try different names (maybe nickname vs real name)
- If stuck, ask someone

### ✓ When Applying:
- Use your real name
- Use a real email
- Ask parent/guardian for help
- Keep your password safe!

### ✓ If Something Goes Wrong:
- **Refresh the page** (pull down and wait)
- **Clear cache** (close all tabs, open again)
- **Ask for help** - use the Help button!
